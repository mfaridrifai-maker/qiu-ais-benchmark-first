BOOTSTRAP DEFINITIONS (manuscript Sections 2.8 and 2.9)
=======================================================
Uniform stability protocol (Table 3; revision/stability_uniform.py)
  1. Draw 200 samples of N = 314 respondents with replacement (RandomState(42)). Duplicated respondents enter the fit with their multiplicity; no restriction to unique or out-of-bag respondents is made.
  2. Re-estimate the min-max scaling of the four composites on the resample.
  3. Refit the method on the resample (k = 3 for Table 3): K-Means (k-means++, n_init = 5), fuzzy c-means (m = 2, up to 200 iterations, tolerance 1e-6, one random start), Ward hierarchical clustering (cluster centroids of the resample), Gaussian mixture (full covariance, n_init = 2).
  4. Assign all 314 ORIGINAL respondents to the resulting clusters (scaling from the resample): nearest centroid for K-Means, fuzzy c-means, hierarchical; the fitted model's prediction for the mixture.
  5. ARI between that assignment and the same method's full-sample partition (computed on the original sample, scaling from the full sample). The common comparison set is therefore the 314 original respondents.
  Results (mean ARI, SD): K-Means 0.907 (0.062), fuzzy c-means 0.955 (0.034), hierarchical 0.822 (0.090), mixture 0.673 (0.220); K-Means k = 2, 3, 4: 0.945, 0.907, 0.781.
Silhouette intervals and the paired difference against tertiles (confidence_intervals.py)
  1,000 resamples; K-Means refitted on each resample (n_init = 5) with the min-max scaling re-estimated; the Silhouette of the resample partition and the Silhouette of the equal-sized tertile split of the overall score are computed on the SAME resample, and the interval is the percentile interval of their difference (paired).
Threshold sensitivity (200 resamples): K-Means on the overall score alone is refitted in each resample and the two boundaries and the Less Satisfied share are recorded.
Why fuzzy c-means and K-Means have the same full-sample labels but different stability: not tested. A plausible reason is that soft memberships move fewer respondents near the boundaries between resamples; the manuscript states this as an untested explanation.
